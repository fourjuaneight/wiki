The **search for extraterrestrial life** divides into two largely separate enterprises with different institutions, budgets, and failure modes. **Biosignature** work looks for chemical or morphological traces of biology, whether microbial, ancient, or otherwise, and is carried out mainly by government space agencies through robotic missions costing billions. **Technosignature** work, historically called **SETI**, looks for artifacts of technology such as narrowband radio beacons or nanosecond laser pulses, and runs on private philanthropy at a fraction of the cost. The two fields share personnel and journals but almost no funding.

Neither has produced a confirmed detection. Every candidate to date has either dissolved under reanalysis or been explained by an abiotic mechanism, and the field's most useful discipline is the speed with which it now kills its own claims. This entry covers what the evidence actually supports, the conspiracy narratives that have grown alongside it, the missions and instruments currently operating, and the fiction that shaped public expectation of all of it.

## What we know

### Planets and ingredients are abundant

The census problem is solved. NASA's exoplanet archive passed 6,000 confirmed planets in September 2025, and Kepler occurrence-rate work places roughly four rocky habitable-zone planets around Sun-like and K-type stars within 10 parsecs. Organic molecules are similarly ubiquitous, detected on Mars, in Enceladus's plume, in comets, and throughout the interstellar medium. Neither real estate nor raw chemistry is the bottleneck.

### The grading problem

Because "potential biosignature" is routinely laundered into "life found" by secondary coverage, NASA proposed a **Confidence of Life Detection** (CoLD) scale, a seven-level framework for reporting evidence of life beyond Earth, intended to communicate progress rather than binary discovery.[^green2021] Nothing has ever advanced past the lower rungs. Any claim reported as confirmed should be checked against the primary literature first.

### Mars: the strongest candidate

In September 2025 the Perseverance team reported results from the Bright Angel formation in Neretva Vallis, on the western edge of Jezero Crater. Organic-carbon-bearing mudstones there contain submillimetre nodules and reaction fronts enriched in ferrous iron phosphate and sulfide, likely **vivianite** and **greigite**, in textures the science team informally nicknamed "poppy seeds" and "leopard spots."[^hurowitz2025] On Earth these minerals form as byproducts of microbial iron and sulfate reduction, and they are considered candidate mineralogical biosignatures.

The paper is careful about what it does not claim. It examines the null hypothesis, that low-temperature abiotic reactions produced the same suite, and finds real obstacles to it: abiotic sulfate reduction is kinetically inhibited below roughly 150–200 °C, and no hydrothermal sulfide source is evident in the surrounding stratigraphy. But it also notes that organic-driven abiotic reduction of ferric iron is documented between 10 °C and 80 °C, and that further analysis is required. The conclusion is that the formation contains features that *warrant consideration* as potential biosignatures, and that resolving their origin requires returning the core sample, named Sapphire Canyon, to a terrestrial laboratory.[^hurowitz2025]

That sample is still on the rover.

### Ocean worlds

Enceladus provides the cleanest ocean-world data. Reanalysis of Cassini's E5 flyby, the fastest of all Enceladus encounters, at 17.7 km s⁻¹, identified aliphatic, cyclic ester and alkene, and ether or ethyl moieties in ice grains sampled minutes after ejection, plus tentative nitrogen- and oxygen-bearing compounds.[^khawaja2025] Freshness is the point: grains collected directly from the plume cannot be explained by space weathering in the E ring, which implicates the subsurface ocean and hydrothermal chemistry at the seafloor.

The counterweight arrived the same year, when laboratory work presented at EPSC-DPS 2025 showed radiation-driven chemistry could produce comparable molecules on icy surfaces without any biology. This is the recurring structure of the entire field: an abiotic mimic exists for nearly every organic signal.

### K2-18b and the failure mode

The sub-Neptune K2-18b demonstrates how biosignature claims collapse. JWST near-infrared data yielded methane at 5σ and carbon dioxide at 3σ, interpreted as supporting a hydrogen-rich atmosphere over a liquid-water ocean, with a low-significance dimethyl sulfide signal alongside. A 2025 mid-infrared follow-up reported features consistent with **dimethyl sulfide** (DMS) and dimethyl disulfide at 3σ and argued they constituted independent biosignature evidence.[^luque2025]

Independent reanalysis dismantled it. A joint analysis of the full 0.6–12 µm spectrum across three reduction pipelines and two retrieval codes found insufficient evidence for either molecule, concluding that marginal preferences arose from limiting the number of candidate molecules and from oversensitivity to small differences between data reductions. Ethane, a common product of methane photolysis with a nearly identical absorption structure, fits the data just as well.[^luque2025]

Two further problems undercut DMS as a biosignature in principle. It has been detected on comet 67P, establishing abiotic production. And no line lists exist for it at pressures below 1 bar, so retrievals rely on cross-sections measured at 278 K in a 1-bar nitrogen atmosphere and applied at all temperatures and pressures, which systematically overestimates absorption.[^luque2025]

The lesson generalizes: 3σ is not detection, and reanalysis is where claims die.

### Technosignatures: sensitive nulls

Every major SETI survey has returned nothing, but the nulls are quantitative and therefore useful. A Green Bank Telescope search of 61 transiting TESS targets of interest across 1–11 GHz found no surviving events after interference removal, constraining transmitters brighter than a few hundred terawatts to fewer than 52%, 20%, 16%, and 15% of those targets at L, S, C, and X band respectively.[^franz2022] A later Parkes survey of 27 eclipsing exoplanets processed 1,954,880 signals, of which 14,639 passed automated filters, all attributable to terrestrial radio frequency interference.[^barrett2025] An archival reanalysis of the Breakthrough Listen Green Bank data set produced tighter limits still.[^painter2025] In the optical band, VERITAS analyzed thirty hours of dedicated observations covering 136 targets plus 249 archival observations covering 140 more, and found no pulses consistent with a technosignature.[^acharyya2023]

Taken together, these results say that persistent high-power beacons in the searched volume are rare, brief, or beamed elsewhere.

### The Wow! signal

The 1977 **Wow! signal** remains the most cited SETI candidate and is now most plausibly natural. The Arecibo Wow! project reanalyzed unpublished Ohio SETI archive data and proposed that a sudden brightening of the hydrogen line in cold interstellar clouds, triggered by a transient source such as a magnetar flare or soft gamma repeater, via maser flaring or superradiance, accounts for the signal's narrow bandwidth, duration, intensity, lack of modulation, and non-repetition.[^mendez2024] A second paper revised its properties: a peak flux density exceeding 250 janskys, more than four times the commonly cited figure, at 1420.726 MHz, from a position more precisely determined and slightly shifted from earlier estimates.[^phl2026]

As of August 2026 the project's position is that the evidence for a genuine astrophysical event is compelling, while the source and mechanism remain unidentified. The authors do not claim artificial origin is conclusively ruled out.

## Conspiracies

The gap between "no confirmed detection" and popular belief is filled by a durable body of claims that the answer is already known and withheld.

### Crash retrieval and reverse engineering

The U.S. Department of Defense investigated its own history and found nothing. The All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) assessed that claims of a government programme reverse-engineering extraterrestrial technology largely trace to circular reporting among a consistent group of individuals active in UAP matters since at least 2009. Material alleged to have come from an off-world craft was analyzed and determined to be ordinary and terrestrial. Named defense contractors denied on the record ever possessing such material, and the KONA BLUE proposal, a prospective, never-approved special access programme, assumed the existence of material that had never been collected.

The sighting data is similarly mundane. AARO's fiscal 2024 report logged 757 new reports during the period against roughly 1,650 total holdings, with resolved cases attributed to balloons, birds, uncrewed aerial systems, satellites, and aircraft; 444 cases were placed in an active archive for lack of sufficient data to analyze at all. The report states plainly that AARO has discovered no evidence of extraterrestrial beings, activity, or technology, and separately that it has no indication the reported incidents are attributable to foreign adversaries.[^aaro2024]

The pattern is old. Project SIGN, Project GRUDGE, and the 1953 Robertson Panel reached the same conclusion across seventy years, the panel specifically citing the complete absence of recovered hardware.

### Roswell, abductions, and ancient astronauts

The 1947 Roswell debris was Project Mogul, a classified array of high-altitude balloons built to detect Soviet nuclear tests, a cover story concealing a terrestrial secret. Accounts of recovered alien bodies entered the narrative in the late 1970s, three decades after the event.

The abduction phenomenon maps closely onto sleep paralysis with hypnopompic hallucination: paralysis, chest pressure, a sensed presence, humanoid figures. Recovered-memory hypnosis, the standard investigative technique in the field's formative period, reliably generates confident false detail. No physical evidence has emerged in roughly seventy years of claims.

The **ancient astronaut** thesis, that the pyramids, the Nazca lines, and Puma Punku required non-human assistance, rests structurally on the assumption that ancient non-European peoples could not have built them. Every named site has documented quarries, tool marks, and in several cases unfinished works that preserve the construction sequence.

More recently, the "Nazca mummies" presented to the Mexican Congress in 2023 were identified by Peruvian forensic authorities as assemblages of looted human and animal bone joined with adhesive.

### Interstellar objects

The 2025 discovery of 3I/ATLAS, the third known interstellar object, generated a technological-origin hypothesis advanced most prominently by Avi Loeb. NASA classifies the object as a comet on spectral grounds, coma, gas tail, and water-driven activity, and its non-gravitational acceleration is consistent with ordinary outgassing from a nucleus of normal size. Multiple independent radio campaigns, including Breakthrough Listen and the Allen Telescope Array, found no technosignatures. The episode is a useful illustration of anomaly counting: a sufficiently long list of individually unremarkable properties can be assembled into an apparent improbability.

## Current scientific efforts

### Technosignature programmes

The SETI Institute is the only U.S. organization dedicated wholly to the search for life and intelligence beyond Earth, and runs three parallel programmes.[^seti2026] The **Allen Telescope Array** at Hat Creek was the first radio telescope designed from the ground up for SETI, and now serves largely as a rapid-response instrument for targets of opportunity. **COSMIC**, the Commensal Open-Source Multimode Interferometer Cluster, is installed on the Very Large Array and takes a parallel copy of the data stream from each of the 27 antennas, analyzing it in real time for signals of technological origin while the telescope does unrelated astronomy. Because it is commensal, it costs essentially no telescope time, and its projected reach is tens of millions of stellar systems.[^seti2026]

**LaserSETI** covers the optical band, watching for pulses lasting nanoseconds. The physics favors it: a pulsed laser can briefly outshine its own star, so a transmitting civilization could signal cheaply. The instruments use slitless spectroscopy, which spreads incoming light into a full spectrum so natural and artificial sources produce easily distinguished signatures, and they require no pointing; the network stares at everything continuously. Fifteen instruments across seven sites give roughly 58% sky coverage, with Southern Hemisphere expansion planned.

Breakthrough Listen, based at UC Berkeley and funded privately, remains the largest single programme, observing with Green Bank, Parkes, MeerKAT, FAST, Jodrell Bank, and the Automated Planet Finder, with all data released publicly.

### Mars

Perseverance left Jezero Crater in late 2024, ascending the western rim into terrain that predates the crater itself, among the oldest exposed rock in the solar system. It carries 23 sealed core samples aboard, with a backup set of ten deposited in a surface depot. Curiosity, approaching fifteen years of operation, continues climbing Mount Sharp through progressively younger strata, where boxwork ridges record groundwater circulating through bedrock fractures later than models predicted.

The unresolved question is retrieval. **Mars Sample Return** ran roughly $6 billion over budget with a projected return date in the 2040s, and the FY2026 appropriation formally closed the programme office with zero dollars allocated. Congress redirected $110 million to a new Mars Future Missions line intended to develop common technologies for robotic and eventual crewed Mars missions, sample return among them; the samples themselves are not abandoned.[^kiraly2026] The strongest potential biosignature ever recovered therefore sits in a titanium tube on a rover with no funded vehicle to collect it.

ESA's **Rosalind Franklin** rover now carries proportionally more weight. Scheduled to launch in October 2028 and land at Oxia Planum in 2030, its distinguishing capability is a two-metre drill feeding an onboard sterile laboratory, reaching beneath the depth at which radiation and oxidants have destroyed surface organics for billions of years.[^cnes2025] Despite being zeroed in the FY2026 White House request, Congress restored NASA's contribution at $73.9 million.[^kiraly2026]

### Beyond Mars

Europa Clipper, launched in 2024, takes an Earth gravity assist in December 2026 and reaches Jupiter in 2030. It is deliberately a habitability mission rather than a life-detection mission. Dragonfly, a nuclear-powered rotorcraft bound for Titan, launches in 2028 and asks a different question: whether prebiotic chemistry works in liquid methane rather than water. ESA's JUICE arrives at Jupiter in 2029 en route to Ganymede orbit.[^nssdc2026]

Enceladus, arguably the best target in the solar system, has no funded mission; the leading ESA concept would not arrive until around 2050. On the exoplanet side, the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope carries a coronagraph that serves as the technology demonstration for the Habitable Worlds Observatory, the only proposed instrument capable of directly characterizing an Earth analogue's atmosphere. Congress funded Roman at $300 million and the Habitable Worlds Observatory at $150 million for FY2026, both far above the requested levels.[^kiraly2026]

## Pop culture influences

Fiction has done more than science to fix public expectation of what contact would look like, and the most durable works split cleanly into three modes.

The **invasion mode** descends from H. G. Wells's *The War of the Worlds* (1898) through *The Thing from Another World* (1951), *Invasion of the Body Snatchers* (1956), and *Independence Day* (1996). Its concerns are political rather than cosmological; the alien is a vehicle for anxieties about conformity, infiltration, or national vulnerability. *V* (1983) contributed the reptilian-infiltrator premise that later migrated wholesale into real conspiracy belief.

The **civilization mode** treats aliens as populations with cultures and politics. *Star Trek* (1966) established the template, *Star Wars* (1977) made the cantina, aliens as background density rather than plot event, a genre default, and *Mass Effect* (2007) turned alien diplomacy into a game mechanic. Ursula K. Le Guin's *The Left Hand of Darkness* (1969) and Octavia Butler's *Dawn* (1987) use alien biology to interrogate gender and consent respectively.

The **unknowable mode** is the rarest and ages best. Lovecraft's *At the Mountains of Madness* (1936) established indifferent deep-time entities; Stanisław Lem's *Solaris* (1961) built an entire novel around the impossibility of communication; the Strugatsky brothers' *Roadside Picnic* (1972) proposed that aliens visited, never noticed us, and left litter, a premise that seeded *Stalker* and the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. games. Kubrick's *2001: A Space Odyssey* (1968) made unseeable intelligence commercially viable, Peter Watts's *Blindsight* (2006) separated intelligence from consciousness, and *Arrival* (2016) made language itself the obstacle. Liu Cixin's *The Three-Body Problem* (2008) popularized dark-forest reasoning to the point where it now appears unattributed in discussions of METI risk.

Two works had measurable effects outside fiction. *The X-Files* (1993) is the single largest driver of the modern grey-alien-and-cover-up narrative in popular belief, and Whitley Strieber's *Communion* (1987), marketed as non-fiction, put the grey face on paperback racks worldwide and materially accelerated the abduction wave of the late 1980s. *Ancient Aliens* (2010) performs the same function for the ancient-astronaut thesis, with reach far exceeding its evidentiary basis.

Games followed a parallel track. *Space Invaders* (1978) built the arcade industry, *Metroid* (1986) named a genre, *X-COM: UFO Defense* (1994) reframed invasion as a resource-management problem, and *Halo* (2001), *Dead Space* (2008), *Alien: Isolation* (2014), and *Subnautica* (2018) each refined a distinct emotional register, militarized, visceral, predatory, and awestruck respectively.

The third mode is worth dwelling on, because it is the one the science supports. If anything is out there, the evidence so far suggests it will not be shaped like our expectations, and the searches that have narrowed the parameter space most usefully are the ones that returned nothing at all.
