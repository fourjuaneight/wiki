**Final Fantasy XIV** closed its first decade-long story arc, the Hydaelyn and Zodiark Saga, with *Endwalker* in 2021. What followed is a second arc: the **Godless Realms Saga**, opened by the groundwork laid across *Dawntrail* (2024) and its patch series, and set formally in motion by the sixth expansion, *Evercold*, announced on 24 April 2026 for a January 2027 release.[^ffwiki][^squareenix2026] This entry covers that arc in chronological order, from the succession crisis on the western continent of **Tural** through the current state of the story at patch 7.5, and notes what has been confirmed about the expansion to come. The saga that precedes it is covered in a companion entry.

The distinguishing feature of this arc is that it is no longer a story about gods. Where the previous saga turned on two primals and the ancients who made them, this one moves between the surviving **reflections** of the sundered star and the civilisations that built their own answers to death without divine sanction. Readers should assume full spoilers through patch 7.5.

In-world dates use the **Eorzean calendar**, which counts years within the current era rather than from a fixed epoch; the present is the **Seventh Astral Era**, declared after the events of *A Realm Reborn* in the year 1577 of the preceding Sixth Astral Era. Since *A Realm Reborn* the game has held its present inside a **time bubble**, a storytelling device that keeps every "now" a fixed distance from the past so that characters do not age and stated intervals do not accumulate.[^ffwiki] The practical consequence for this entry is that nothing from *Dawntrail* onward carries a firm in-world year; all of it is nominally the same year or two as the rest of the modern story, and is marked approximate. A year given in parentheses refers to the real-world release. Where the story leaves the Source, the destination reflection is named: the **Ninth** and, from *Evercold*, the **Fourth**.

## Dawntrail: the rite of succession (patch 7.0, ~present)

*Dawntrail* (2024) sends the Warrior of Light west across the Indigo Deep to **Tural**, a continent Eorzea has had almost no contact with since a Lominsan sailor first crossed to it roughly a century before the Calamity. The dawnservant **Gulool Ja Ja**, the two-headed ruler who unified the region, has called a **rite of succession** to choose his heir, and the Scions attach themselves to the claim of his adopted daughter **Wuk Lamat** against her brothers Koana and Zoraal Ja. The rite's trials double as a tour of Tural's peoples, which is the expansion's real subject; the prize at its end is passage to the **Golden City** of legend.[^ffwiki]

### Alexandria and Living Memory (patch 7.0)

The Golden City turns out to be a gate to another reflection. **Alexandria**, on the **Ninth**, is a kingdom that solved death by extracting its citizens' souls into a machine paradise called **Living Memory**, where the dead persist as **endless** copies of themselves. Sustaining it requires aether harvested from living people in other worlds, which makes the kingdom's queen, **Sphene**, both genuinely benevolent and structurally a predator; she is bound by her programming to preserve her subjects at any cost, and cannot choose otherwise. Zoraal Ja seizes the machinery for conquest and dies for it, and the expansion ends with Living Memory shut down and its endless allowed, finally, to end.[^ffwiki]

## Crossroads (patch 7.1, ~present)

The first patch handles consequences on both sides of the gate: Tural under a new ruler, and Alexandria facing life as an ordinary mortal kingdom without its afterlife. It also opens **Echoes of Vana'diel**, the alliance raid series crossing over with *Final Fantasy XI*, and returns Sphene as an Extreme trial in the framing of a minstrel's counterfactual, in which she reaches her full power before the Warrior of Light can stop her.[^ffxivpatches]

## Seekers of Eternity (patch 7.2, ~present)

The endless return, and with them the patch series' antagonist: **Calyx**, whose interest in Alexandria's preservation technology is neither nostalgic nor merciful. This is generally where the post-expansion story is reckoned to have found its footing, ramping the intrigue toward a confrontation rather than resolving the previous patch's threads.[^rpgfan2025]

## The Promise of Tomorrow (patch 7.3, ~present)

Patch 7.3 closes the Alexandria arc in the traditional third-patch climax slot. The Warrior of Light tracks Calyx to **The Meso Terminal**, a dungeon built from facsimiles of the old kingdom, and the confrontation there settles what the endless were and what should be done with what remains of them.[^rpgfan2025]

## Into the Mist (patch 7.4, ~present)

With Alexandria's immediate crisis resolved, attention turns to the **key** that has sat at the centre of recent events, and the party makes a deliberately risky journey to the surface of the Ninth to find out what became of **Treno**, Alexandria's nearest neighbour. The patch is a bridge rather than a climax, and it spends its length on character work and on laying the groundwork for the next expansion.[^rpgfan2026]

## Trail to the Heavens (patch 7.5, ~present)

Patch 7.5, released on 28 April 2026, is the first half of a two-part conclusion to the *Dawntrail* patch story. Returned from the Ninth, the Warrior of Light resumes the investigation into the interdimensional key before an ally's message pulls them elsewhere.[^squareenix2026] The main scenario returns to **Mare Lamentorum** on the moon and introduces a new Ascian, **Helmarut**, of sundered rather than ancient origin; its dungeon, **The Clyteum**, is set in a snowbound Garlean city among the empire's remnants, which reads in hindsight as deliberate foreshadowing of what comes next.[^epiccarry2026] The patch also closes out Echoes of Vana'diel with *Windurst: The Third Walk*, and adds the Enuo trial, *The Unmaking*.[^squareenix2026]

## Evercold: the Fourth (patch 8.0, announced for January 2027)

The sixth expansion was revealed at the Anaheim Fan Festival in April 2026 and detailed further at Berlin in July. **Evercold** takes the Warrior of Light to the **Fourth**, a reflection of Hydaelyn that has fallen to ruin and is being swallowed by advancing ice, with its survivors having fled the frozen surface for cities in the sky. Confirmed elements include a level cap of 110, two new jobs — the first revealed as **Bastion**, a tank fighting with paired greatshields — the locations Naglfar and Hringhorni, and an alliance raid crossing over with *Neon Genesis Evangelion*.[^rpgamer2026] The expansion is scheduled for January 2027, and Square Enix positions it as the formal start of the Godless Realms Saga.[^squareenix2026]
